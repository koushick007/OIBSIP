The craft

Made by people who type for a living.

We started MOTIF because the keyboards we could buy always compromised somewhere — the case, the switches, or the sound. So we built our own in a small shop, one CNC pass at a time.

Each case is machined from a solid billet of 6063 aluminum, bead-blasted, and anodized in-house. Switches are hand-lubed and tested before a single board leaves the bench.

  • Individually numbered and inspected
  • Two-year warranty on every board
  • Fully programmable via open firmware
